Not sure which class is right for you? Choose what you want from your yoga practice and we'll recommend one.

Yoga Classes

Gentle Yoga

A slower-paced class focused on comfortable movement, stretching, and relaxation. This class is a great choice for beginners and older adults.

Vinyasa Yoga

A flowing yoga class that connects movement with breathing. Students can adjust movements based on their experience and comfort level.

Restorative Yoga

A relaxing class focused on gentle poses and mindful breathing to help reduce stress and encourage relaxation.

Private Sessions

Private yoga sessions provide personalized instruction based on your goals, experience level, and individual needs. Sessions are available by appointment.
