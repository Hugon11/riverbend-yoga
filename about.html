Meet Our Instructors

Emma Carter

Emma is a yoga instructor who enjoys helping beginners feel comfortable and confident in their practice. Her classes focus on mindful movement, breathing, and creating a supportive environment for every student.

Daniel Lee

Daniel teaches vinyasa and restorative yoga. He encourages students to work at their own pace and modify poses based on their individual comfort and experience.

Accessibility

Riverbend Yoga Studio is committed to creating an inclusive environment. Students are encouraged to discuss accessibility needs with an instructor before class so that appropriate modifications and accommodations can be provided.

Studio Policies

  • Please arrive 10 minutes before your scheduled class.
  • Wear comfortable clothing that allows easy movement.
  • Silence mobile devices during class.
  • Respect the comfort and personal space of other students.
  • Let your instructor know about any accommodations you may need.
